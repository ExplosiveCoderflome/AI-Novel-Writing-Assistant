The Short Drama Workbench is used to extend novel content into short drama structure, character relationships, scene segments, and episode materials. It is suitable for use after the novel has clear appeal points and character conflicts.

<Info>
  Comic and short drama are best used after the novel's main plot, characters, and chapter content are established. If the novel's protagonist goals, conflicts, and world rules are not yet clear, return to Auto Director and chapter preparation first.
</Info>

## When to Open It

You can enter the Short Drama Workbench in the following situations:

- You want to judge whether a novel can be adapted into a short drama.
- You want to organize chapter content into episode segments.
- You want to strengthen hooks, twists, and scene conflicts.
- You need to prepare materials for short video or short drama adaptation.
- You want to extract faster-paced expressions from the novel's main plot.

## Relationship with Novel Content

The Short Drama Workbench should derive from novel content, not replace the novel main plot.

It can use:

- Novel synopsis.
- Character relationships.
- Generated chapters.
- Key conflicts.
- World rules.
- Reader payoff points and appeal.

After generating short drama materials, you still need to confirm whether they conform to the original novel's settings. Short drama adaptation will strengthen pacing and conflicts, but cannot arbitrarily destroy character motivations.

## Recommended Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a Novel">
    Choose a work with clear conflicts and appeal points from existing projects.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Chapters or Plot Segments to Adapt">
    Clarify the chapter range to process today.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Extract Core Conflicts and Character Goals">
    Organize the core confrontation and protagonist demands in this segment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Break into Short Drama Scenes or Episodes">
    Re-segment chapter content according to short drama pacing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Strengthen Opening Hook, Emotional Reversal, and Ending Cliffhanger">
    Give each episode clear appeal points and reasons to keep watching.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Reusable Materials">
    Keep confirmed short drama materials within the project.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The Short Drama Workbench is suitable for periodic derivative work. It is not recommended to do large-scale adaptation before the novel's main plot is stable.

## Quality Points to Watch

Short drama materials should be checked for:

- Whether each episode has a clear hook.
- Whether the protagonist's goal is easy to understand.
- Whether the conflict is expressible on screen.
- Whether the twist is clear.
- Whether the ending gives a reason to keep watching.
- Whether the original novel's core appeal points are preserved.

If the short drama version conflicts with the novel version, prioritize protecting the novel main plot's consistency.

## Relationship with the Creative Main Chain

It is recommended to first complete the novel direction, characters, and chapter foundation before doing short drama adaptation. The Short Drama Workbench can help you extract stronger conflicts from existing content, and can also reveal novel pacing issues in reverse, but it should not become the main entry point for beginners to complete long-form novels.

## Usage Suggestions

First select a chapter with strong conflict as a sample. Check whether the short drama adaptation results can preserve character motivations, world rules, and payoff points, then decide whether to continue expanding to more chapters.
