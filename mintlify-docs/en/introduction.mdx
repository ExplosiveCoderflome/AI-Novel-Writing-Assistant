**Biz Novel Studio** is an open-source AI novel writing workbench. You only need an idea to start; the workbench will help you connect direction, characters, world, and chapters: AI assists with world and character planning, chapter-by-chapter writing, checking, and saving, giving long-form creation a path you can keep walking down.

Long-form novels are continuously advanced by the Auto Director, while short stories follow an independent complete drafting workflow. It is designed for first-time novelists, and also provides finer tools for authors and developers who need to maintain long-term materials and study AI-assisted creation workflows.

**The desktop version is ideal for starting to write directly; the source version is suitable for development and self-hosting.** Models require your own API configuration, and Knowledge Base retrieval can be enabled as needed.
